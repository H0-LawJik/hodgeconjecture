## 1. Is this the statement the literature makes?

Literature: analytification commutes with fibre products, (X ×_S Y)^an = X^an ×_{S^an} Y^an, and for S = Spec ℂ the analytic space of a product is the product of the analytic spaces [`Serre1956` §1 to §2, `SGA1` Exp. XII §1; section and proposition numbers unverified]. On complex points this is a homeomorphism (X ×_ℂ Y)(ℂ) ≅ X(ℂ) × Y(ℂ); for X, Y smooth of dimensions d, e the product of holomorphic charts is a holomorphic chart of the product manifold of dimension d + e.

## 3. Trivially true for a reason unrelated to the mathematics?

Attempts, all failed or excluded:

- Degenerate topology. If upstream's topology were indiscrete or discrete on all complex points, any bijection would be a homeomorphism. It is neither: it is the gluing of pointwise-convergence topologies on Hom(Γ(U), ℂ), and upstream proves `isOpen_overOpen_inter_preimage` with it. The proof uses it honestly: the forward direction is upstream's `continuous_map` twice; the inverse is tested on the subbasis (`continuous_iff_analyticSubbasis` inside `continuous_of_isAffine`), on affine products, after localizing by `openEquiv` (upstream `continuous_openEquiv_symm`) and pushing forward by `continuous_map` of `prod.map` of the open inclusions.
- The no-finiteness claim. It is plausible and true: on affine X = Spec A, Y = Spec B the topology is pointwise convergence on Hom_ℂ(A ⊗_ℂ B, ℂ) ≅ Hom(A, ℂ) × Hom(B, ℂ), and since A ⊗ B is generated as a ring by the images of A and B (`closure_range_union_eq_top`, from Mathlib's `isPushout_appTop_of_isPullback`), pointwise convergence on A ⊗ B is pointwise convergence on A and on B. Products of affine opens cover X ⨯ Y and are open; nothing needs finite type. For schemes not of finite type the analytic topology is an unusual space, and the statement holds for it as defined.
- The atlas. The ω-maximal atlas over ℂ is a proper subset of the partial homeomorphisms (a chart followed by coordinatewise conjugation lies outside it for d ≥ 1; `docs/sketches/affine-chart-analytic.redteam.md` §7b, no Lean test yet). The proof is the honest one: both directions are analytic, the forward one by upstream's `analyticAt_localChart_symm_map` for the two projections, the backward one by `analyticAt_evaluate_prodLift`, which writes each local section of X ⨯ Y as a quotient of polynomials in pullbacks from the factors.
- Unused hypotheses: none. `hφ`, `hψ` feed `analyticAt_chart_map_localChart_symm` and `analyticAt_evaluate_symm_of_mem_maximalAtlas`; the two smoothness instances fix the charted spaces of the factors and, through `smoothOfRelativeDimension_prod`, that of the product.
- Empty index types, `ℕ∞` coercions: none; d = 0 or e = 0 gives a zero block, harmless.
- `pointProdEquiv` alone is the universal property of the product; it is the expected content of the bijection and not a defect.
