## Findings

1. **The statement is the literature's, with stronger hypotheses.** Literature: a compact manifold has finitely generated integral homology [`Hatcher2002` Appendix, Corollaries A.8 and A.9; numbering unverified], and over the field ℚ, H^n(X; ℚ) ≅ Hom_ℤ(H_n(X; ℤ), ℚ), the Ext term vanishing because ℚ is injective over ℤ [`Hatcher2002` §3.1, Theorem 3.2; numbering unverified]. So dim_ℚ H^n(X(ℂ); ℚ) = rank H_n(X(ℂ); ℤ) < ∞. The literature fact holds for every complex algebraic variety; the rung asks for smooth, projective and integral, which is what T1 uses.

4. **The injectivity lemma, step by step.** Kernel-checked; what follows checks that it proves the right thing.
   - **Well-defined on classes.** `Ψ₀` is defined on cycles of the forgotten dual complex D and descends through `descHomology` once `toCycles ≫ Ψ₀ = 0`, which is `cochainEval_d_iCycles_rat`: a coboundary δe evaluates to e(∂z) = 0 on an integral cycle z, through `cochainEval_dualMap_d`. When `i + 1 ≠ n` the differential is zero by `shape`, which is the degree-0 case of the descent.
   - **Factorization through H_n(ℤ).** For a cocycle c (`hc`: c ∘ ∂_{n+1} = 0, from `iCycles_d` and `forgetDual_d_apply`), `F = eval(c) ∘ iCycles` vanishes on integral boundaries (`cochainEval_d_eq_zero` with `prev ℕ n = n + 1`) and descends to a ℤ-linear `G : H_n(Y; ℤ) → ℚ`. `G` vanishes on the generators sᵢ = [zᵢ], so `G = 0` by `LinearMap.ext_on_range`, and c vanishes on every integral cycle. This is the map H^n(C^•(Y; ℚ)) → Hom_ℤ(H_n(Y; ℤ), ℚ) of the universal coefficient theorem, followed by restriction to generators; restriction to a generating set is injective on Hom.
   - **The ℚ Baer step.** A faithful copy of `exists_dualMap_d_eq_of_cochainEval`: the restriction φ of f to integral (k+1)-chains vanishes on ker ∂ (`hf`), so it factors through im ∂ ⊆ C_k(Y; ℤ) (`QuotientAddGroup.lift`, `quotientKerEquivRange`), extends to g : C_k(Y; ℤ) → ℚ by `Module.Baer.of_divisible ℚ`, and `dualOfFun` turns g on simplices into a rational cochain e with δe = f, checked on simplices through `dualToFun_injective`. ℚ is divisible, so the extension is legitimate; this is the vanishing of Ext_ℤ(H_{n−1}(Y; ℤ), ℚ) at chain level.
   - **Degree 0.** The upward complex has no coboundary into degree 0, so H⁰ is the cocycles. Every 0-chain is a cycle (`Kℤ.shape 0 0`, then `exists_cycles_of_d_eq_zero`), so c vanishes on every integral simplex and c = 0 by `dualToFun_injective`. The witness is v = 0, and `iCycles` is mono, so w = 0.
   - **Coefficient bookkeeping.** The rational cochains are `Module.Dual ℚ (C_n(Y; ℚ))` and are evaluated on `C_n(Y; ℤ)` through `cochainEval` = `intChainsLift (dualToFun c)`; that is the identification Hom_ℚ(C_n(Y; ℚ), ℚ) = Hom_ℤ(C_n(Y; ℤ), ℚ) on the simplex basis, with no scalar.
